## 📄 Spielbeschreibung (Englisch — Primary)

### ⚔️ Master the Realm. Forge Your Deck. Defeat Your Rivals.

**Raid Realms** is a tactical 1v1 deckbuilder that combines the positional battlefield mechanics of classic card battlers (*Hearthstone*, *Magic: The Gathering*) with the real-time drafting decisions of in-match deckbuilders (*Dominion*, *Star Realms*).

Choose from **10 distinct fantasy races**, each featuring unique starter decks, signature mechanics, and game-changing racial passives.

---

### 🔥 Key Features
- **10 Asymmetric Races:** Command Humans, Orcs, Elves, Undead, Dragons, Dwarves, Fairies, Demons, Werewolves, or Vampires.
- **Dynamic In-Match Market:** Buy new units, destructive spells, and resource buildings every round to counter your opponent's build.
- **Deep Combat Keywords:**
  - **TAUNT:** Forces enemies to attack this defender first.
  - **FORTIFY:** Protects your hero against direct damage until buildings fall.
  - **CHARGE:** Attack immediately on summon.
  - **LIFESTEAL:** Heals your hero for damage dealt.
- **Multiple Game Modes:**
  - 🤖 **Solo PvE:** Duel against smart AI agents with "Normal" and "Tactical (Hard)" difficulty settings.
  - ⚔️ **Local Pass & Play:** Play against a friend on the same screen or tablet.
  - 🌐 **Online 1v1 (P2P WebRTC):** Instant direct matches via room code or invite link. Zero registration, zero server lag.
- **100% Local-First & Privacy-Focused:** No tracking cookies, no server databases. Works completely offline as an installable PWA!

---

### 🕹️ How to Play
1. **Main Phase:** Play resource cards, summon units onto your 7-slot board, erect buildings, and draft upgrades from the market.
2. **Combat:** Declare target attacks with your ready units against enemy troops, fortifications, or directly at the enemy hero.
3. **End Turn:** Collect base income (+3 Gold), activate building generation, and pass initiative.
